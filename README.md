# Hometown Tire & Auto Repair — Demo Website

A demo marketing site built for **Hometown Tire & Auto Repair**, a neighborhood tire and auto shop in downtown Plant City, FL. Built by Wilson Innovations as a demo-first sales pitch.

## Business
- **Address:** 203 E Baker St, Plant City, FL 33563
- **Phone:** (813) 652-8093
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8 AM – 5 PM; closed weekends
- **Google:** 4.5★ from 140 reviews
- **Services:** tires & wheels, rotations & balancing, oil changes, general repair

## Design
- **Fonts:** Crete Round (slab-serif display) + Karla (grotesque body), via Google Fonts
- **Palette:** brick red, denim blue, and cream — a warm, hometown Plant City Americana look
- **Layout:** sticky header with always-visible call button, denim-blue hero over a small-town repair scene, facts strip, six-card services grid, two alternating tire/repair spotlights, a denim trust band, real Google-review testimonials, a brick call-to-action band, and a downtown location/hours block.

## Notes
- `noindex` meta is present while this is a demo — remove it (see the comment in `<head>`) when the site goes live.
- Single self-contained `index.html`; no build step, no external JS. Works from `file://`.
- Open Graph / Twitter card images use absolute URLs for link previews.
- Images: Unsplash (auto/tire topical), lazy-loaded.
- Structured data: `TireShop` LocalBusiness JSON-LD with real rating, hours, and reviews.
